Help

Trouble connecting? Use Chrome on Android with Bluetooth on (and Location, on Android 11 or older). Press the Crafty's button once to wake it, and close the S&B web app or any other phone connected to it.

Drag the ring or tap − and + to set the temperature. The page keeps to the Crafty's own 40–210 °C range. Boost and superboost can only be started with the button on the Crafty. Bowl and battery figures are estimates that learn from how you use it.
