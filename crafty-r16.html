Help

Trouble connecting? Use Chrome on Android with Bluetooth on (and Location, on Android 11 or older). Press the Crafty's button once to wake it, and close the S&B web app or any other phone connected to it.

Drag the ring or tap − and + to set the temperature. The page keeps to the Crafty's own 40–210 °C range. Boost and superboost can only be started with the button on the Crafty. Battery figures are estimates that learn from how you use it.

Strains. Tap the strain line on the Bowl tile to pick what's in the bowl. Each strain keeps its own batches, each with a THC %, a date and a photo, and learns its own pace from your taps, starting from your overall pace. Adding a strain by name looks it up on StrainAPI (only the name is sent) to fill in effects, terpenes and flavors, which you can edit. Strains and photos stay on this phone.

Bowl meter. Each segment is a temperature level, 360° to 410°. Time at temperature fills the level you're at and every level below it, faster when it's hotter, so a bowl that's spent at 365° still gives vapor at 390°. The Crafty doesn't report your draws, so this is an estimate: tap No more vapor when a level runs out (or Still vapor when it hasn't) and the meter corrects this bowl and learns your pace.

Profiles

Profiles

A Follow the bowl profile starts low and steps up one level (5°F) each time the bowl meter says the level you're at is spent, so you get flavor first and the rest later. Tap Not yet on a step to go back down. Setting the temperature yourself pauses it.

Aim for a terpene

Sets the temperature near where that terpene boils. Flower always gives off a mix, so this leans the flavor, it doesn't isolate one.
